I've been thinking about Catherine and this new role, and honestly I'm not sure how to balance everything.

The opportunity is a big deal. More responsibility, more visibility, better compensation, and the kind of work I've wanted to be doing for a while. I'm excited about it. I also know it's going to mean more travel and less predictable hours. I don't want to pretend otherwise.

Catherine and I are exclusive now, and I really do like her. I'm attracted to her, obviously, but it's more than that. She's incredibly smart, she's funny, she has her own career and opinions, and I like that she challenges me. I don't feel like I have to entertain her or pretend to be someone I'm not. We can disagree about something and still have a great night. That's not something I take for granted.

But I know the communication thing is an issue. I'm not a huge texter. Sometimes I get into a production and genuinely don't look at my phone for hours. Other times I see a message, think I'll respond when I have a minute, and then suddenly it's 10 PM. I don't necessarily think that means I'm not interested. When we're together, I'm present. I remember what she tells me, I ask questions, I make plans, and I want to spend time with her. I guess I assume that should count for something.

I also know that from her perspective, it might feel different. She has mentioned that consistency matters to her. I don't want her sitting around wondering whether I'm interested. But I also don't want to feel like I have to be in constant communication to prove that I care. I'm not sure where the reasonable middle ground is.

The other thing is that I do want a family eventually. Marriage, kids, all of that. I'm not saying I need to do it tomorrow, and I'm not ready to make promises about a timeline when we've only recently become exclusive. But I don't want to build a career that leaves no room for the life I say I want.

I suppose I'm worried that I'll end up disappointing her because I'm not naturally good at balancing all of this. And I don't know if accepting this opportunity makes that more likely. I don't want to turn down something important to me just because a relationship is getting serious, but I also don't want to keep telling myself that I'll figure it out later.

I think part of me assumes that if the relationship is good, we'll work around my schedule. Maybe that's unfair. I don't know. I haven't really figured out what I'm actually willing to change versus what I just hope she'll understand.

I really don't want Catherine to feel like an afterthought. But I also don't want to promise her a version of me that I can't realistically deliver.
