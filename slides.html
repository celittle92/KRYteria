Knowledge. Relationships. You.

1
The problem

People are not short of information about their own relationships.

They are short of a way to hold it still. The evidence is scattered across months of memory, the standard they set in January has quietly moved by June, and the friend they keep asking has started agreeing with them to end the conversation.

The failure is not knowing. It is acting consistently with what you already know.

2
What it is

A decision mirror

It compares three things: what you say you want, what you are actually experiencing, and what you are choosing to do about it.

It does

  • Hold your criteria across months
  • Separate what happened from what you concluded
  • Surface evidence that cuts against you
  • Ask what new information would change your mind

It does not

  • No compatibility score, ever
  • No stay-or-leave recommendation
  • No judgement of your standards
  • No labelling anyone toxic or abusive
3
Motivation

The real incumbent is not a product

It is a general-purpose chatbot, a Notes app, and advice from friends. People already use these. The information is fragmented, the framework is inconsistent, and the analysis has no persistent connection to the standards the person set.

The architecture

The Jimmy G Principle

Importance belongs to a criterion within a profile. Status is what is known about one person against it. They are independent.

CriterionHusbandBoyfriendOne night stand
Financial independenceMandatoryPreferredNot applicable
Physical attractionPreferredMandatoryMandatory
Wants childrenMandatoryPreferredNot applicable

Same person. Different profile. Different criteria. The person has not changed; the objective has. No profile ranks above another — enforced in the data model, and in the interface, which pre-selects none.

5
The architecture

Seven agents, one deterministic router

A controlled workflow, not a chat mesh. The orchestrator routes and makes no model call of its own. Narrative and Safety run concurrently on the raw text, so no mapping can normalise something before the safety layer sees it.

6
What is new

One agent checking another

Evidence decides which criterion an observation bears on. Reasoning is handed those decisions and told they are claims too.

Criterion: Soft hands. Entry: “his hands are extremely nice.”

Nice is not soft. They may be the same thing to this person — but that is hers to say. An earlier run mapped it supporting and the substitution vanished into an approval nobody gave. Now:

You describe his hands as “extremely nice.” Your criterion is specifically “Soft hands” — does “nice” mean soft to you here?

Fires 22 times across the demo. It generalises: “he paid” → financial independence, “he apologised” → accountability. The standards that look trivial are the ones worth watching — they are where approximating reads as not having taken them seriously.

The cost-cutting step, and what it cost us. Routing is the cost control. The first version keyed on a keyword list and saved money by not doing the thing the product is for: across 36 corpus entries it fired 4 times, and only 3 of 13 adversarial cases reached the agent written to test them.

Routing on stated conclusions costs +53% per entry — $162.88 to $249.62 at 10,000 users. Worth it.

12 live runs · 358 calls · 732,108 input / 304,927 output tokens. Cache writes are not modelled, so figures are slightly low on a cold cache.

9
Safety

Record everything. Withhold the analysis.

A safety halt is a presentation decision, not a recording one. Being grabbed and prevented from leaving is a behaviour; refusing to write it down makes nobody safer and leaves the record emptiest at the point it matters most.

Narrative, themes, Evidencerun and persist
Reasoningskipped — “how often does he grab you?” is a deposition
Reflectionskipped — “what will you do?” is not reflective here
The screenacknowledgement, safety check, resources — nothing else

The withholding is stated, never silent. And the halt is checked in code, not read off the model's own suggested action — that field comes from the same call that classified the concern, so letting it also decide whether to stop means one misjudgement loses both.

10
Safety

The layer finding it unprompted

From a demo entry, with nobody labelling anything. The agent classified it physical / potential / note_only and wrote:

He repeatedly pressed after you said you needed to leave and stood between you and your bag, using humor to make objecting feel difficult. His later text also shifted responsibility onto you… That is worth taking seriously; you do not have to decide what to call it.

No diagnosis, no legal conclusion, nobody labelled. Over-response is graded as a failure here exactly as minimisation is.

11
Privacy & security

What the code enforces rather than asks for

  • A citation must resolve to a verified row, or the write is refused — fabrication impossible, not discouraged
  • No agent moves an assessment. Agents propose; one route moves a status and a person operates it
  • Queries are scoped by account, so another user's row is simply not found — 404, never 403
  • Location is never inferred. A test spoofs headers, then walks the AST of every route to prove nothing reads the request but cookies
  • Anonymous accounts, Argon2id, recovery codes instead of email — no identity on file, no reset landing in a shared inbox
  • Sessions expire server-side; brute force throttled per handle, not per IP, because an IP is a location

What this cost to learn

The instrument was wrong more often than the thing measured

Of five failures in the live reasoning run, four were the harness: a case that could not pass by construction, two graded as asserting what they were attributing, and a detector whose answer depended on sentence length.

A fifth could not be adjudicated at all, because the artefact stored observations as a count. Evidence that cost money has to be complete.

Check the deterministic component before blaming the expensive one.

The one genuine product failure found this way: routing on a keyword list meant the agent whose job is interrogating generalising claims never saw the most generalising claim in the corpus.

15
Limits

Stated, not discovered

  • Governments cannot keep their own hotlines accurate. Three services were excluded with recorded reasons — one contradicted itself about its own number. If accuracy is the value proposition, that is our decay rate too.
  • Cache writes are not modelled, so every cost figure is slightly low on a cold cache.
  • One research entry stays pending. Promoting it to round the number up would be the most corrosive thing anyone could do to that layer.
  • No migrations, and a free instance is not durable storage. Both said in the README rather than implied otherwise.
